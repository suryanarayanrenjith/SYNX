## 1.0.6

### Weather that comes and goes
- Rain is now weather rather than a constant. Every race and every Free Roam
  tour gets its own forecast: usually dry, sometimes a road still wet from a
  shower that has just passed, and now and then drizzle, a shower or a storm
  that rolls in mid-race and clears again.
- Each route has its own climate. The coast and the city see the most rain,
  the canyon and the desert the least, and the roads dry faster in the desert.
- Free Roam no longer rains most of the time: rain within the first five
  minutes of a tour went from about half of all tours to about one in five.
- Storms bring distant thunder. There is no lightning flash.
- Chapter 3 is still a storm, but now it surges and eases instead of holding
  one level. Chapter 4's golden-hour opening always stays clear.

### Aurora Forge
- The factory floor is dry. It no longer shows wet patches, damp gloss or
  standing water, and a car that drives in out of the rain in Free Roam
  arrives on a dry floor.

### Driving
- Brushing a barrier is now a scrape instead of a crash. At racing speed, a
  shallow hit costs a few per cent of your speed and slides you along the
  wall, where it used to spin the car and cost a third of your speed. Square
  hits are still full crashes.
- "LEAD LOST" no longer appears in the first second of a race when the cars
  are side by side off the line.

### The opening
- The loading screen, the speedometer intro and the safety notice run much
  more smoothly, especially on low-end PCs. The 3D scene no longer renders
  behind them.
- The speedometer intro is reworked: a needle that bounces off its stop pin,
  shift lights, a rev bar, a peak marker, a punch and a ring of light on every
  gear change, and a camera that dives into the dial at the end.
